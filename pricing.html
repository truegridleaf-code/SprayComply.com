Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any setup fees?

No, there are absolutely no setup fees or hidden costs. You just pay the flat monthly rate for your chosen plan.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No, SprayComply is a month-to-month service. You can cancel at any time without penalty.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Yes, you can easily change your plan at any time right from your account dashboard.

What if I have more than 10 applicators?

If you need more than 10 applicators, contact us for an Enterprise plan tailored to your fleet's needs.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial on all plans. No credit card required to start!

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export all your spray logs to a PDF or CSV file at any time. We recommend doing this before cancelling.
