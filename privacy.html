Privacy Policy

How we handle and protect your information

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SprayComply collects information to provide better services to our users. We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and billing information when you register for an account. Additionally, as part of the core functionality of our service, we collect GPS location data, pesticide application records, and associated client information that you input into the system.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve our services. This includes processing transactions, generating compliance reports, syncing NOAA weather data automatically, providing customer support, and communicating with you about updates or changes to our service.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on servers located in the United States. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal information and application records from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers to facilitate our services. This includes:

  • Weather APIs: We integrate with NOAA to fetch automatic weather data based on your location at the time of a spray log.
  • Payment Processors: We use Stripe to process secure payments. Your payment details are securely handled by Stripe and are not stored on our servers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. You may update your account details through the app settings or by contacting our support team. If you choose to delete your account, we will securely erase your personal data in accordance with our retention policies, subject to any legal obligations.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience, analyze site traffic, and support marketing efforts. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of our site may not function properly as a result.

7. Children's Privacy

SprayComply is intended for use by businesses and professionals. Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us
