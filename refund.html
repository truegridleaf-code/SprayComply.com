Refund Policy

Our fair and transparent refund policies

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. 14-Day Free Trial

We offer a 14-day free trial for all new users to fully experience SprayComply. No payment information is required to start the trial, and you will not be charged during this period unless you explicitly choose to subscribe to a paid plan.

2. Monthly Subscriptions

For users on monthly billing plans, you may cancel your subscription at any time. When you cancel, you will not receive a refund for the current billing cycle, but your service will remain active until the end of the paid period. We do not provide refunds for partial months of service.

3. Annual Subscriptions

For users on annual billing plans, we offer a prorated refund if you choose to cancel within the first 30 days of the subscription start date. After the first 30 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable, but your service will continue until the end of your annual term.

4. How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through the billing section of your account dashboard, or by emailing our support team at hello@spraycomply.com. Upon cancellation, all recurring charges will stop immediately.

5. Exceptions

In rare circumstances, such as a major service outage or billing error, SprayComply may issue a refund or credit at our sole discretion. These exceptions do not establish a precedent for future refunds.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your billing or our refund policy, please reach out to us:
