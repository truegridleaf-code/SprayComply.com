About SprayComply: Austin, Texas Pesticide Compliance Software

Built by Texas licensed structural pest control applicators for small fleet operators.

Born in Austin, Engineered for Texas Pest Fleets

SprayComply was founded in Austin, Texas, by licensed pest control operators who experienced firsthand the frustration of compliance audits. Under Texas Structural Pest Control Service (SPCS) and Texas Department of Agriculture (TDA) regulations, missing a single weather reading or EPA registration number can result in severe citations and license scrutiny.

Managing paper logs on truck clipboards led to water-smeared records, while billion-dollar enterprise CRMs forced small businesses into $2,000 onboarding fees and rigid multi-year contracts. Texas operators needed an agile sidecar tool that would log chemical applications in 25 seconds, fetch real-time NOAA meteorological conditions via GPS, and export certified TDA Form Q527 PDFs on demand.

Today, SprayComply supports residential and commercial pest control operators across Austin, Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston, and San Antonio - ensuring audit immunity without disrupting their existing scheduling or invoicing software.
